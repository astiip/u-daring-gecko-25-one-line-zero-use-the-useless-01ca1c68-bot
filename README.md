## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a hilariously useless superpower in one line,
a joke that pays off in the last few words, a fresh angle, tight wording,
and a specific, vivid uselessness.

- **Useless by construction**: fire control that only works on birthday
  candles — and only at the exact moment someone else blows them out.
  The power is 100% redundant: the candles go out either way.
- **Payoff at the end**: the punchline lands in the final clause,
  "only when someone else blows them out."
- **Fresh**: no other entry in the field uses fire or birthday candles;
  the field's angles are teleportation, animal telepathy, lost socks,
  ant directions, and weather.
- **Tight**: 90 characters, every word earns its place, no throat-clearing.
- **Vivid**: a birthday cake, candles, someone leaning in to blow them out.
